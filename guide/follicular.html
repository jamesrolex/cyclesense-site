The cycle, explained for partners · Energy rising

Energy is rising

After a period, oestrogen climbs. Many people feel brighter, more energetic and more social.

Ways to help

  • Suggest a plan: a date, a walk, something new.
  • A good week to talk through bigger things.
  • Try something active together.

Avoid: Don’t assume. Ask what your partner fancies doing.

Plans that tend to suit this week

  • Try a new restaurant
  • A day trip or a long walk somewhere new
  • A class or activity together
  • Book something for next week
