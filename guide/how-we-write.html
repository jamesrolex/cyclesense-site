How we write these guides

  • General, not personal. The guides describe how cycles, pregnancy and menopause commonly feel. They are never about anyone’s own health data.
  • Based on public health guidance, mainly the NHS. Each guide links the pages it draws on.
  • No diagnosis, no medical advice. If something worries you, talk to a doctor.
  • Plain, kind English. No blame, no “it’s just hormones”.
