Pregnancy, explained for partners

The third trimester

Getting comfortable is hard. Sleep is broken, breathlessness and swelling are common, and the to-do list keeps growing.

Ways to help

  • Take on errands and chores without being asked.
  • Help with the practical prep, so your partner doesn’t have to hold it all.
  • Keep evenings calm and comfortable.

Avoid: Don’t pile on plans or visitors.

General guidance, not medical advice. If anything worries you both, contact your midwife or doctor.
