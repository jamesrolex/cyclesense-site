Cycle Sense

Your cycle, shared your way

A calm, private place for your cycle, sleep and mood. And a short, kind daily note for the people you choose, saying only what you want them to know.

For you

Understand your day

  • Which week of your cycle it is, in plain English.
  • Sleep, rest and heart rate, compared only with your own usual, from the Health app.
  • Mood and symptoms in a tap. A gentler Today when it's a rough day.
  • Every life stage: regular or unpredictable cycles, contraception, trying for a baby, pregnancy, after a loss, after birth, perimenopause, after menopause, or no cycle at all.

For the people you choose

Share only what you choose

  • Up to five people: a partner, parent, son or daughter, sibling or friend.
  • You review every note before it goes. Everything else is off until you turn it on.
  • They get ideas for how to help, and can send back a reaction, a check-in or a small promise. No chat, by design.
  • Pause everyone, or remove anyone, at any time. Nobody is told.
The cycle, explained for partners

Private by design

No account. No server. No ads.

Your health data stays on your iPhone. Shared notes travel through your own iCloud, end-to-end encrypted, and are deleted after 48 hours. There's no analytics and nothing to sign up for.

Cycle Sense is not a medical device. It doesn't diagnose or treat any condition and must not be used for contraception.

Get it

Cycle Sense is in testing on iPhone and will be on the App Store soon.
