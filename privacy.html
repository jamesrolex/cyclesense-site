Privacy Policy

Cycle Sense · Last updated 9 October 2026

Cycle Sense is published by James Coop, an individual developer in the United Kingdom. “We” and “the developer” below mean James Coop.

The short version: Cycle Sense has no account and no server. Your health data stays on your iPhone. We (the developer) never receive it, cannot see it, and do not sell or share it.

What the app reads

With your permission, Cycle Sense reads from Apple Health: menstrual cycle data, symptoms, sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleeping wrist temperature, workouts (including effort scores), steps, VO₂ max and State of Mind (mood). It uses this only on your iPhone to show your cycle, your body signals and fitness compared with your own usual, patterns over time, and to draft optional notes for the people you choose.

If you turn on “Lunar” (moon phase, with astrology just for fun), you can add your birth date, and optionally time and city. The city comes from a list built into the app, so nothing is looked up online. These details stay on your iPhone.

If your iPhone supports Apple Intelligence and you leave “Polish notes” on, your note is reworded by Apple’s on-device model. It runs entirely on your iPhone, never sees your health data, and nothing is sent to Apple or to us.

What the app writes

Moods and symptoms you log in Cycle Sense are saved to Apple Health on your iPhone, so Health stays your single record.

What your people see (only if you choose to share)

You can share with up to five people (a partner, parent, son or daughter, sibling or friend). Each person joins with a one-time code and gets their own private space in Apple iCloud (CloudKit), end-to-end encrypted. Nobody sees anyone else’s.

  • Everyone: only the short note you review and send, and your first name. Notes are deleted automatically after 48 hours.
  • A partner, only if you choose: which week of your cycle it is (like “Pre-period”), and optionally what’s coming next (like “Period likely in about 3 days”). You choose this per person and can turn it off at any time; it is removed straight away.
  • A partner, in perimenopause or after menopause, only if you choose: just that you’re in that stage, so they get ideas for how to help. Never your symptoms or logs.
  • Family and friends: never anything about your cycle.
  • Optional, each time you send: a one-line body summary such as “slept less than usual”. Never numbers.

Web link (optional, for someone without an iPhone): your note, and the week guide if you allow it for a partner, is encrypted on your iPhone with a key that exists only in the link you send. Apple stores only scrambled data it cannot read, and neither can we. The web page is read-only. Anyone who has the link can read your latest note until you turn the link off, so send it only to that person. Turning it off deletes the note from iCloud straight away.

People you share with can send back reactions, check-ins, how they’re feeling and small pledges; you see these on your iPhone. Nobody ever sees your cycle dates, symptoms, measurements or moods. You can remove anyone at any time; they lose access straight away.

If someone shares with you: your own sleep, heart, fitness and mood data (if you connect it) is read from Apple Health and stays on your iPhone. Moods you share are also saved privately to your own Health, if you allow it. None of it is sent to anyone; the person who shares with you only sees the mood you choose to send. You can leave their space from Settings; anything you sent them (reactions, check-ins, moods, pledges) is deleted first.

What we collect

Nothing. Cycle Sense contains no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs and sends no data to the developer. Health data is never used for advertising or sold.

Subscriptions

Cycle Sense Premium is bought through Apple. Apple handles payment; we never see your name, email or card, and buying it changes nothing about what data the app reads or shares.

Crash reports

iOS may collect technical crash and performance reports (no health data or note text). They stay on your iPhone unless you choose to share them from Privacy › Diagnostics, or you have turned on “Share with App Developers” in iOS Settings, in which case Apple shares anonymised crash reports with us.

Deleting your data

In the app, Privacy › “Stop sharing and delete everything” ends everyone’s access and erases every note, reaction, log, report and your birth details from your iPhone and from iCloud. If someone shares with you, Settings › “Delete everything” does the same on your side. Your Apple Health data is not touched; you manage it in the Health app.

Not medical advice

Cycle Sense shows patterns in your own data. It is not a medical device, does not diagnose or treat any condition, and must not be used for contraception.
