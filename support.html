Getting started

Open Cycle Sense, choose “I track my cycle” and allow Apple Health access. Your cycle, sleep and heart signals appear on Today once Health has a little data. Nothing leaves your iPhone unless you send a note.

Sharing with someone

  1. Go to People › Add someone, pick who they are (partner, parent, son or daughter, sibling or friend) and what they can see.
  2. Show them the 6-digit code. It works for 15 minutes.
  3. On their iPhone they install Cycle Sense, choose “Someone shared with me” and type the code.

Both phones need to be signed in to iCloud with iCloud Drive on.

Common questions

They stopped getting notes. Make sure both phones are on the latest version from the App Store and signed in to iCloud. If it still doesn’t work, open People › their name › Show pairing code and have them type the new code. They stay connected; nothing is lost.

A new phone. Install Cycle Sense on the new phone and sign in to the same iCloud account. If someone shares with you, ask them for a new code.

Change what someone sees. People › their name. Changes apply straight away.

Remove someone. People › their name › Remove. They lose access immediately.

Delete everything. Privacy › “Stop sharing and delete everything”. Your Apple Health data is not touched.

Not medical advice

Cycle Sense shows patterns in your own data. It is not a medical device and must not be used for contraception. If you’re worried about your health, talk to a doctor or other health professional.
